Before you start

When someone is stuck in their rank, there is always a reason. Most of the time it is not the one wrong comp but the fundamentals. The better they get, the higher you climb.

These are exactly the basics I look at first in coaching – and with this plan you can check them yourself.

1What I look at first in a replay

Stage 1 and the augment pick on 2-1. More is decided here than most players think.

  • Are the right units being held? If you buy and sell randomly in Stage 1, you have no clear board on 2-1.
  • Is the board judged honestly? How strong am I really – is it enough for a win streak or not?
  • Are 4 gold thrown away on 2-1? Leveling for no reason, just to play one more unit, and then losing anyway. I see it all the time.
  • Does the augment fit the board? Strong board → tempo. Weak board → econ and lose streak. Not something in between.

Scouting only comes after that for me. It is more for players who are already a bit better – you have to know what you are looking for, otherwise it does not help you.

Your checkWatch only Stage 1 and 2-1 of your last three games. Did you know every time whether you were playing for tempo or econ?

2The three checks when someone is stuck

Check 1Your game plan

What is your plan in the game – and does it fit the lobby?

  • Do you want to play a comp no matter what?
  • Are you playing a line that is highly contested and does not work right now because you do not get the units early?
  • Or are you playing a semi-optimal line even though there would be something better?
Your checkAfter every game, write down in one sentence what your plan was and when you had it. If it says the same comp five times, you are forcing.

Check 2Your items

  • Do you slam at all? Many players hold components on the bench without building them – and then simply do not have the power.
  • Do you split them right? Carry items on the carry, tank items on the tank, not scattered everywhere.
  • When do you slam? Building too late wastes power just like building wrong.
Your checkWatch a game where you played for tempo: were components sitting on your bench while you lost rounds? Then you wasted power. In a lose streak you may hold them – at your rolldown at the latest (4-2 in Fast 8) you slam everything.

Check 3The right decisions for your comp type

Every play style has its own rules – reroll, Fast 8 and Fast 9 work completely differently.

  • Augments: do they fit your comp type?
  • Roll timings: do you roll when your play style calls for it?
  • Level-ups: do you level to win fights – or just because you have gold?
  • Tempo: do you even have enough tempo to go Fast 9?
Your checkPick one play style for a week and read the matching guide on tftclimb.com. After every game, check: did I roll and level on the timings it says?

3The smallest change that gets you LP fast

Play more for tempo – and do not be afraid to slam items.

I very often see players slam only the items that are in a guide. Instead of thinking for themselves: could my unit not carry a different item pair too? That gets you stuck.

Especially in Fast 8 you can slam lots of different items early. Spirit Visage or Warmog's Armor, an early Deathblade, Infinity Edge or Giant Slayer – that is not bad in the early game as long as it does not break your item economy. And stay flexible afterwards: just because you slammed a certain item, you do not have to force your way into one line.

Your checkWhen you play for tempo in your next games, slam your first item as soon as you have a usable pair – even if it is not the perfect item from the guide.

4The biggest mistake in your rank

Iron to Silver

You play without a plan

You do not understand the game yet, and that is completely fine. Learn the basic mechanics: gold and interest, leveling, items, positioning.

Your check: After a game, can you explain why you rolled on which level?

Gold and Platinum

You play a comp blindly

Comp picked, augments without a plan, zero flexibility. And you often do not know why something else would be better right now.

Your check: Ask yourself with every augment: why this one – and what is my board missing?

Emerald and Diamond

Items and augments are not clean yet

Items are split and slammed badly. An item augment on 2-1 without a good board, or a level you do not need – then you miss the interest later. With three comps you play confidently, you still reach Master.

Your check: Did you level on 2-1? Then check whether you really won the two rounds after.

Master and up

You lack full flexibility

Reading your spot correctly and deciding which line you can play. From Grandmaster on, every level gets questioned: do I level on 2-5? Do I need level 7 on 3-5?

Your check: After every game, write down which two lines you could have played on 3-2.

5Your plan for the next 5 games

  1. Decide on 2-1: tempo or econ – and stick with it.
  2. No 4 gold for a level that does not win you a fight.
  3. Playing tempo: slam the first item as soon as you have a usable pair.
  4. In a lose streak: hold components – and slam everything at your rolldown.
  5. One sentence after every game: what was my plan, and since when?
